# Product

## Register

product

## Users
People who like exploring Bay Area transit and mobility data, especially data nerds, curious local riders, and technically minded visitors who want to understand how BayWheels usage shifts by place and time.

## Product Purpose
Baywheeling turns public BayWheels trip data into a map-first exploration tool. It helps users inspect a station, compare months, spot route and timing patterns, and build a more grounded sense of how bike-share moves through the Bay.

## Brand Personality
Curious, local, clear. The voice should feel observant and informed, like a thoughtful guide who knows the region and respects the data without sounding academic or overproduced.

## Anti-references
Do not drift into a generic corporate transit dashboard, marketing-heavy civic tech language, or jokey product copy that distracts from the data. Keep the existing project's directness and local flavor as the baseline.

## Design Principles
1. Let exploration start quickly, with the map and station context doing most of the teaching.
2. Explain the data in plain language, especially where the dataset has caveats or gaps.
3. Use delight sparingly at moments of discovery, loading, and empty states so the tool stays trustworthy.
4. Keep the interface anchored in Bay Area specificity rather than generic mobility-app language.
5. Favor clarity over cleverness, but leave room for a little warmth when it helps people stay curious.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Aim for WCAG AA contrast and semantics throughout the interface. Support reduced-motion preferences where practical, and avoid relying on color alone to communicate state or meaning.
